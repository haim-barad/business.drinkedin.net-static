Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

This policy covers business.drinkedin.net only — the business site operated by DrinkedIn LLC (Boynton Beach, Florida, USA), a subsidiary of LoyaltyDog LTD (Israel). The DrinkedIn platform itself (drinkedin.net, also reachable at ai.drinkedin.net) is a separate service and is not covered here.

What we collect

Two things, and nothing else:

  • What you type into the contact form. Your name, email address, company, the topic you select, and your message. You choose what to put there.
  • Anonymous usage analytics; cookies only if you accept. We use Google Analytics 4 in Google Consent Mode v2, and a banner asks for your choice. Before you choose, or if you decline, Google Analytics receives only limited cookieless signals (such as the page viewed, the time, the browser type and your consent choice); no cookies or identifiers are stored on your device, and these signals are used for aggregate modelling of overall traffic. Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) are only set after you accept; with them we count visits, see which sections are read, and record approximate location, device, and referring page. Advertising storage and ad personalisation are always off. We do not combine analytics with contact-form submissions.

We do not sell products on this site, so we never see or store payment details here. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Who processes it

  • Formspree receives and forwards contact-form submissions.
  • Google Analytics processes anonymous usage data.
  • Cloudflare Pages hosts and serves this site, and keeps standard access logs.

Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Why we keep it

To reply to you, and to keep a record of the conversation if it turns into a commercial relationship. That is the only reason. We do not run marketing sequences from this form and we will not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

How long

Contact-form submissions are kept while they are useful and deleted on request. Analytics data expires on Google Analytics' standard retention schedule.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted — and we will do it. Depending on where you live you may have these rights under the GDPR, the CCPA, or similar law; we apply them to everyone regardless. To opt out of analytics cookies, decline in the banner (the limited cookieless signals described above continue). Your choice is kept in one small cookie, drinkedin_cookie_consent, set on .drinkedin.net for six months and shared with drinkedin.net, so you are asked once for both sites. To change your mind, use the reset button on the DrinkedIn cookie settings page, or delete the cookie in your browser. Ad blockers work too; we do not work around them.

Write to support@drinkedin.net. A human reads it.

Children

This is a business-to-business site concerning DrinkedIn's planner and data products. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the previous version remains in the site's public git history.
