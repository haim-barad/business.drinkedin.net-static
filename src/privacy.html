Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 July 2026

This policy covers business.drinkedin.net, the business site operated by DrinkedIn LLC (Boynton Beach, Florida, USA), a subsidiary of LoyaltyDog LTD (Israel). The consumer site at drinkedin.net and the agent platform at ai.drinkedin.net are covered by their own terms.

What we collect

Two things, and nothing else:

  • What you type into the contact form. Your name, email address, company, the topic you select, and your message. You choose what to put there.
  • Anonymous usage analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which sections are read. This sets cookies and records approximate location, device, and referring page. We do not combine it with contact-form submissions.

We do not sell products on this site, so we never see or store payment details here. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Who processes it

  • Formspree receives and forwards contact-form submissions.
  • Google Analytics processes anonymous usage data.
  • Cloudflare Pages hosts and serves this site, and keeps standard access logs.

Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Why we keep it

To reply to you, and to keep a record of the conversation if it turns into a commercial relationship. That is the only reason. We do not run marketing sequences from this form and we will not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

How long

Contact-form submissions are kept while they are useful and deleted on request. Analytics data expires on Google Analytics' standard retention schedule.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted — and we will do it. Depending on where you live you may have these rights under the GDPR, the CCPA, or similar law; we apply them to everyone regardless. To opt out of analytics, use your browser's do-not-track or an ad blocker, both of which we respect by not working around them.

Write to privacy@drinkedin.net. A human reads it.

Children

This is a business-to-business site concerning a platform for AI agents. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the previous version remains in the site's public git history.
